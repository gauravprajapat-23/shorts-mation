## Marketplace behavior

The templates are inserted as global `is_default=true`, `visibility='public'` website templates. Users do not press a Starter/install button and therefore do not create duplicate private rows.

## YouTube quality note

These templates are designed to support substantial original editing and storytelling: multiple scenes, original narration slots, media/B-roll slots, source/evidence areas, pattern interrupts, progress states, comparisons, reveals and conclusions.

A template by itself cannot guarantee YouTube monetization or prevent a low-effort/repetitious-content classification. Users should still provide original media, narration/commentary, factual value, meaningful variation between videos and appropriate rights to all media.
